# PR 04 — Research protocol, case study and reproducible owner analysis

## Why

A portfolio project needs to explain the research choices, product tradeoffs, evidence, and limitations, not merely show a live website. Analysis claims must be reproducible and separate from a pretty chart.

## Changes

- `methodology.html`: a readable public product/research case study explaining the hypothesis, design, limits and commercial follow-up.
- Review and finalize `docs/RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md` (introduced in PR 01). Its start/end dates must already be committed before v2 starts; do not backdate them.
- `analysis/analyse.py`: owner-only CSV analysis with exact Welch t confidence interval when SciPy is available. Never uploads or prints participant IDs.
- Overhauled README with setup gates, tests, privacy and versioning.

## Acceptance

- Public methodology doesn't claim results that have not been collected.
- A CSV export from `V2_Responses` produces arm means and medians and a valid Welch interval if >=2 per arm and SciPy is installed.
- The v1 and v2 datasets are clearly labeled and never pooled silently.
- Owner has filled in start/end dates in protocol before v2 goes live.
