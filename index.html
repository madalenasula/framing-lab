Behind the experiment

Same object.
Different context.

A simple behavioural experiment exploring reference-price anchoring and stated willingness to pay.

  1. 01
    Two reference prices

    Visitors are randomly shown CHF 45 or CHF 95. The bottle, description and question remain unchanged.

  2. 02
    One response

    Each person enters their stated maximum willingness to pay in CHF. The averages and medians can then be compared.

  3. 03
    A hypothesis, not a verdict

    This is an exploratory, self-selected online experiment. It cannot establish a representative population effect on its own.
