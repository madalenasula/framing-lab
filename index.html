How the experiment works See study status
Behind the experiment

Same object.
Different context.

We test whether a price shown next to an identical product changes stated willingness to pay. Price may also convey product-quality information; the experiment cannot distinguish these mechanisms.

  1. 01
    Two reference prices

    Visitors are randomly shown CHF 45 or CHF 95. The bottle, description and question remain unchanged.

  2. 02
    One response

    Each person enters their stated maximum willingness to pay in CHF. The averages and medians can then be compared.

  3. 03
    A hypothesis, not a verdict

    This is an exploratory, self-selected hypothetical shopping experiment—not a test of real purchases, a representative population, or proof that any individual was anchored.
